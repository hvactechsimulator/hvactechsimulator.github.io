HVAC Tech Simulator

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

HVAC Tech Simulator is a free HVAC troubleshooting game at hvactechsimulator.com, run by HVAC to the Future. This page explains what the game collects, why, and how to get it deleted. The short version: only what it needs to run accounts, the leaderboard, and basic play counts. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads.

If you play as a guest

You don't need an account to play practice calls. Your settings, progress, and call record are saved in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device.

The game also counts plays so we can see how it's used: when a page is opened, when a call starts and ends, the call type, the score, and whether the device is a phone, tablet, or computer. These counts are tied to a random ID stored in your browser, not to your name, email, or IP address.

If you create an account

  • Leaderboard name and an optional shop or city. These are public on the leaderboard.
  • Password, if you choose one. Only a one-way scrambled version (a bcrypt hash) is stored. Nobody, including us, can read your password.
  • Recovery code, stored the same scrambled way, so you can reset a forgotten password.
  • Your game results: practice call scores and daily challenge results, so your record follows you to any device.

If you sign in with Google

Google tells us your Google account ID, name, and email address so we can log you in. We use them only for signing in. Your email is never shown on the leaderboard or to other players, and we don't email you. We don't get access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive, or anything else in your Google account.

Who handles the data

  • Supabase hosts the accounts, leaderboard, and play counts.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website and, like any web host, may keep standard server logs.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" sign-in, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts provides the game's typefaces.

We don't sell, rent, or share your information with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

Cookies and local storage

The game uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and to save settings and progress. It doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

Email hvactechsimulator@gmail.com from the address on your Google account, or with your leaderboard name, and we'll delete your account, scores, and sign-in records. To clear guest data, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Children

The game is meant for HVAC technicians, students, and people learning the trade. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
